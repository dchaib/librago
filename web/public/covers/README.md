# Demonstration covers

`demo-1.svg` through `demo-4.svg` are original geometric illustrations created for the Librago loans preview. They contain no downloaded artwork, real book jackets, embedded fonts, or external resources. They are fictional collection designs reused across the mock titles, not reproductions of published editions.

These four assets are dedicated to the public domain under CC0-1.0 and may be redistributed, modified, and used without attribution. They are demonstration assets only; real cover retrieval and management are outside this increment.
